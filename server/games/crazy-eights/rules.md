# Crazy Eights

Match the top card by suit or rank, and play an Eight to change the suit. First to empty their hand wins.

## Objective

Be the first to play all of your cards.

## Players

2–7 players.

## Cards and ranking

One standard 52-card deck. Ranks don't beat each other: you only need to **match**. Eights are wild.

**Card points** (for scoring): 8 = 50, J/Q/K = 10, A = 1, other cards = their number.

## Setup / dealing

- 2 players: **7 cards** each. 3 or more players: **5** each.
- The next card is turned up to start the discard pile. If it's an Eight, it is buried in the stock and another card is turned.
- The rest of the deck is the face-down **stock**.
- The player left of the dealer goes first.

## How a turn works

1. **Play** one card that matches the top card's **suit** or **rank**. For example, on the 7♥ you may play any heart or any Seven.
2. **Eights are wild:** play an 8 at any time and **call a new suit**. The next player must match that suit (or play another 8).
3. **Can't play (or don't want to)?** **Draw** from the stock. The host decides how many draws you get:
   - **1 card** (default): draw once, then play if you can, or **pass**.
   - **Up to 3 cards.**
   - **Until you can play**: keep drawing until you draw a playable card.
4. If the stock is empty, the discard pile (except the top card) is shuffled to make a new one.
5. If nobody can play or draw at all, the hand is **blocked**. The player with the lowest card points in hand wins it.

## Scoring

The player who goes out scores the card points left in all other hands.

## Winning

- **One hand (default):** the first player to go out wins.
- **Play to 100 / 200:** keep playing hands until someone reaches the target. The highest score wins.

## Example turns

> The top card is the **7♥**. **Asha** plays the 5♥ (same suit). **Ben** has no hearts and no Fives, so he draws one card, the J♦, which doesn't help, and passes.

> **Chen** plays the **8♠** and calls *"Diamonds!"* Now **Dev** must play a diamond or an Eight. He plays the 2♦.
