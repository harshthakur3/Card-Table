# Go Fish

The classic family game: ask other players for cards and collect sets of four.

## Objective

Collect the most **books**. A book is all four cards of one rank, such as four Sevens.

## Players

2–6 players.

## Cards and ranking

One standard 52-card deck. Only ranks matter: suits are ignored and no rank beats another.

## Setup / dealing

- With **2–3 players** everyone gets **7 cards**. With **4–6 players** everyone gets **5**. The host can fix this at 5 or 7 in the lobby.
- The rest of the deck goes face down in the middle. This is the **pond**.
- If you are dealt four of a kind, it is put down as a book straight away.

## How a turn works

1. **Ask.** Tap a card in your hand to pick its rank, choose another player, and press **Ask**. You may only ask for a rank you already hold.
2. **They have some.** They must give you **every** card of that rank, and you take another turn.
3. **They have none: "Go fish!"** You draw the top card of the pond.
   - **Lucky catch:** if you draw the rank you asked for, you go again. The host can turn this off.
   - Otherwise your turn ends and play passes to the left.
4. **Books.** When you hold all four cards of a rank, they are put down as a book at once.
5. **Empty hand.** If your hand is empty when your turn starts, you draw one card from the pond. If the pond is empty too, you are skipped.

Only you see the card you drew from the pond. Everyone sees cards handed over after an ask.

## Scoring

Each book counts 1. There are no other points.

## Winning

The game ends when all **13 books** are made. The player with the most books wins. Ties are shared.

## Example turns

> **Asha** holds 7♠ 7♥ K♦. She asks **Ben** for Sevens. Ben has the 7♣, so he hands it over. Asha now has three Sevens and asks again.
> This time she asks **Chen** for Sevens. Chen has none: *"Go fish!"* Asha draws the 7♦ from the pond, a lucky catch. All four Sevens make a book, and Asha goes again.

> **Dev** asks Eli for Queens. Eli has none. Dev draws the 3♣, which isn't a Queen, so his turn ends.

## Tips

- If someone just asked for a rank and fished, they still hold that rank. Ask them for it.
- Ask the players with the most cards; they are the likeliest to have what you need.
