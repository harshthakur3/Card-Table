# Hearts

A trick-taking game where you want to take as **few** points as possible. Watch out for the Queen of Spades.

## Objective

Finish with the **lowest score**. Every heart you take costs 1 point and the Q♠ costs 13.

## Players

3–5 players. Four is the classic game.

## Cards and ranking

One standard deck, ranked **A K Q J 10 … 3 2** in each suit. There are no trumps.

- 3 players: the 2♦ is removed, so everyone gets 17 cards.
- 4 players: 13 cards each.
- 5 players: the 2♦ and 2♠ are removed, so everyone gets 10 cards.

## Setup / dealing

All cards are dealt. Before each hand, every player chooses **3 cards to pass**. Everyone chooses at the same time, and nobody sees what they receive until all players have passed.

- With 4 players the direction cycles **left → right → across → no pass**.
- With 3 or 5 players it cycles **left → right → no pass**.

The host can turn passing off.

## How a turn works

1. The player holding the **2♣** leads it to the first trick.
2. Play goes clockwise. You **must follow the suit led** if you can. If you can't, play any card.
3. **No points on the first trick.** On trick 1 you may not play a heart or the Q♠ unless you have nothing else. The host can allow this.
4. **Breaking hearts.** You can't lead a heart until a heart has been played on an earlier trick ("hearts are broken"), unless your hand is all hearts.
5. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, and its winner leads the next one.

## Scoring

At the end of each hand:

| Card | Points |
|---|---|
| Each ♥ | 1 |
| Q♠ | 13 |

**Shooting the moon:** if you take **all 26 points** in a hand, you score 0 and every other player scores 26. The host can turn this off.

## Winning

The game ends when anyone reaches the **target** (100 by default, or 50). The player with the **lowest** score wins.

## Example turns

> **Asha** leads the 2♣. Ben plays the 9♣ and Chen the K♣. Dev has no clubs; he would love to dump the Q♠, but it's the first trick, so he discards the 10♦. Chen's K♣ wins, and Chen leads next.

> Later, Chen leads the 2♠. Dev plays the Q♠ and Asha plays the A♠, taking the trick. Ben has no spades and discards the 7♥. Asha takes 14 points (13 + 1), and hearts are now broken.
