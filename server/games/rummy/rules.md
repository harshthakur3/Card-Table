# Rummy

Classic draw-and-discard Rummy: build sets and runs, then be the first to empty your hand.

## Objective

Be the first to get rid of all your cards by putting them down in **melds**. A meld is either:

- a **set**: 3 or 4 cards of the same rank, such as 8♠ 8♥ 8♦, or
- a **run**: 3 or more cards in sequence in the same suit, such as 4♥ 5♥ 6♥ 7♥.

## Players

2–6 players.

## Cards and ranking

One standard 52-card deck. For runs the order is **A 2 3 … 10 J Q K**: the Ace is low. With the *"Ace can also be high"* option, Q-K-A is also a run. Runs never wrap around (K-A-2 is not a run).

**Card points** (for deadwood): Ace = 1, number cards = their number, J/Q/K = 10.

## Setup / dealing

- 2 players: **10 cards** each. 3–4 players: **7**. 5–6 players: **6**.
- The next card is turned face up to start the **discard pile**. The rest form the face-down **stock**.

## How a turn works

1. **Draw** one card: the top of the stock, or the top of the discard pile.
2. **Meld** (optional, as many as you like):
   - Put down a new set or run from your hand.
   - **Lay off** cards from your hand onto *any* meld on the table, yours or another player's (for example, add the 8♥ to someone's 5♥ 6♥ 7♥).
3. **Discard** one card to end your turn. You can't discard the card you just took from the discard pile, unless it's your last card.

If the stock runs out, the discard pile (except its top card) is shuffled to make a new stock. This can happen up to 3 times per hand; after that the hand ends with no winner.

## Scoring

When a player **goes out** (melds, lays off or discards their last card), the hand ends. They score the card points still left in everyone else's hands.

## Winning

- **One hand (default):** whoever goes out wins. If nobody does, the player with the fewest points in hand wins.
- **Play to 100 / 250:** hands continue, with the deal moving left, until someone reaches the target. The highest score wins.

## Example turns

> **Asha** holds 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ 9♣ 9♦ K♠. The discard pile shows the 9♠, so she takes it. She melds 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ and 9♣ 9♦ 9♠, then discards the K♠. Her hand is empty, so she goes out and scores Ben's deadwood.

> **Ben** draws from the stock and lays off his 8♥ onto Asha's 5♥ 6♥ 7♥, making it 5♥–8♥. He then discards the 2♣.
