# Spades

A partnership trick-taking game. Bid how many tricks your team will take, then make it. Spades are always trumps.

## Objective

Be the first team to reach the **target score** (300 by default; 200 or 500 can be chosen).

## Players

Exactly **4 players** in two teams. Partners sit opposite each other: seats 1 & 3 are **Team A** and seats 2 & 4 are **Team B**.

## Cards and ranking

One standard deck, ranked **A K Q J 10 … 2** in each suit. **Spades are trumps** and beat every other suit.

## Setup / dealing

All 52 cards are dealt, 13 each. The deal moves one seat to the left every hand.

## How a turn works

### Bidding

Starting left of the dealer, each player bids once how many tricks **they** expect to take (1–13). Each team's contract is the total of its two bids.

**Nil:** bid 0 to say you will take **no tricks at all**. It scores ±100 for your team, and your partner's bid is scored separately. The host can turn Nil off.

### Playing

1. The player left of the dealer leads the first trick.
2. You **must follow the suit led** if you can. If you can't, play any card, including a spade.
3. You can't **lead** a spade until spades are broken (a spade has been played to a trick), unless you hold only spades.
4. The highest spade wins the trick. If no spade was played, the highest card of the suit led wins. The winner leads next.

## Scoring

For each team after every hand:

- **Contract made:** 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 point per extra trick. Each extra trick is a **bag**.
- **Contract failed:** −10 points per trick bid.
- **Nil made:** +100. **Nil failed:** −100, and the Nil bidder's tricks count as bags for the team (they don't help the partner's contract).
- **Bags:** every time a team collects **10 bags**, it loses 100 points. The host can turn this off.

## Winning

When a team reaches the target at the end of a hand, the team with the higher score wins. If both teams are tied, another hand is played.

## Example turns

> Asha bids 4 and her partner Chen bids 3, so Team A's contract is 7. Team A takes 8 tricks: 70 + 1 = **71 points** and 1 bag.

> Ben bids **Nil** and his partner Dev bids 3. Ben is forced to win 2 tricks (−100) and Dev takes 3 (+30). Team B scores −100 + 30 + 2 bags = **−68**.
