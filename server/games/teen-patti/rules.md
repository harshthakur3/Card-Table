# Teen Patti

*"Three cards"*, the popular South Asian game of three-card brag. Bet blind or seen, bluff, and ask for a show.

> **Play chips only.** Chips here are just points for fun. They have no value and nothing can be bought, sold or cashed out.

## Objective

Finish the set number of hands with the **most chips**.

## Players

3–6 players.

## Cards and ranking

One standard deck. Card order is **A K Q J 10 … 2** (Ace is high, except in the A-2-3 run).

**Hands, best first:**

| # | Hand | Example | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trail** (three of a kind) | A♠ A♥ A♦ | Higher rank wins; A-A-A is the best hand |
| 2 | **Pure sequence** (straight flush) | 4♥ 5♥ 6♥ | A-K-Q is highest, then A-2-3, then K-Q-J … down to 4-3-2 |
| 3 | **Sequence** (run) | 4♥ 5♠ 6♦ | Same order as pure sequences |
| 4 | **Colour** (flush) | 2♣ 9♣ K♣ | Compare highest card, then the next, then the last |
| 5 | **Pair** | Q♠ Q♦ 3♣ | Higher pair wins, then the odd card |
| 6 | **High card** | A♠ J♦ 9♣ | Compare highest card, then the next, then the last |

Suits never break ties. Hands that are completely equal are a tie.

## Setup / dealing

1. Everyone starting a hand puts the **boot** (ante: 10, 20 or 50 chips) into the pot.
2. Each player is dealt **3 cards face down**.
3. Everyone starts **blind**: they have not looked at their cards.
4. The player left of the dealer bets first. The deal moves left each hand.

## How a turn works

There is a current **stake**, which starts at the boot.

- **See your cards** at any time, even when it isn't your turn. Once you've seen, you are a **seen** player for the rest of the hand.
- On your turn, choose one:
  - **Bet / Chaal.** A **blind** player pays the stake and a **seen** player pays **double** the stake.
  - **Raise.** Pay twice your normal bet, which **doubles the stake** for everyone. The stake is capped at 32 × boot.
  - **Pack** (fold). You drop out of this hand and lose what you've already put in.
  - **Show.** Only when exactly **two players** are left: pay your bet and both hands are revealed. The better hand wins the pot. **If the hands are equal, the player who asked for the show loses.**
- If you don't have enough chips to bet, you may go **all-in** with what you have. All-in players stay in until the showdown. This is simplified: there are no side pots.

## End of a hand

- If everyone else packs, the last player standing **wins the pot without showing**.
- After a **show**, the better hand wins.
- **Pot limit:** if the pot reaches the limit (64× or 128× boot, or no limit), there is an automatic show among everyone still in, and the best hand wins.

Players who can't afford the boot sit out until the game ends.

## Winning

After the chosen number of hands (5, 10 or 20), or when only one player can still afford the boot, the player with the **most chips** wins.

## Example turns

> Boot is 10, so the pot starts at 30 with three players. **Asha** bets **blind 10**. **Ben** looks at his cards (A♠ K♠ Q♠, a top pure sequence!) and **raises**: as a seen player his normal bet is 20, so he pays 40 and the stake becomes 20. **Chen**, still blind, calls 20.

> Asha packs. Ben makes a chaal of 40 (seen pays double the stake of 20). Only Ben and Chen are left, so Chen, still blind, asks for a **show** and pays 20. Ben's A-K-Q pure sequence beats Chen's pair of Sevens, so Ben takes the pot.
