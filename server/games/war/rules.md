# War

Pure luck and suspense: everyone flips a card and the highest card takes them all.

## Objective

Win all the cards. If the host set a battle limit, hold the **most cards** when it is reached.

## Players

2–4 players.

## Cards and ranking

One standard 52-card deck. Ranks from high to low: **A K Q J 10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2**. Suits do not matter.

## Setup / dealing

The whole deck is dealt out face down, and each player keeps their cards as a face-down **deck**. Nobody looks at their own cards.

- 2 players: 26 cards each.
- 3 players: the 2♣ is removed so each player gets 17.
- 4 players: 13 cards each.

## How a turn works

1. **Flip.** Every player presses **Flip** to turn over the top card of their deck. Order does not matter. With **auto-flip** turned on, cards flip by themselves.
2. **Battle.** The highest card wins. Its owner takes every flipped card and adds them, shuffled, to the bottom of their deck.
3. **War!** If two or more players tie for the highest card, there is a war.
   - Each tied player puts **3 cards face down** (the host can set 1–3) and then flips again. Other players sit the war out.
   - The highest new card wins **everything** on the table, including the face-down cards.
   - Another tie means another war.
   - If you don't have enough cards for a war, you put down all but your last card and flip that one. If you can't flip at all, you lose the war.
4. **Out.** A player with no cards left is out of the game.

## Scoring

There are no points. You simply count cards.

## Winning

- The first player to hold **every card** wins.
- If the battle limit set by the host (50, 150 or 400 battles) is reached first, the player with the most cards wins. Ties are shared.

## Example turns

> **Asha** flips the K♠ and **Ben** flips the 5♥. The King is higher, so Asha takes both cards.

> Asha flips the Q♦ and Ben flips the Q♣. **War!** Each puts 3 cards face down, then flips again: Asha shows the 9♠ and Ben the J♥. Ben wins all 10 cards.
